About Anhui Huasen Hydrogen Energy

Taking hydrogen out of laboratories and demonstration parks, into towns and factories that have real waste and real energy demand.

Positioning

One thread, three businesses

Around “biomass / biogas → hydrogen → fuel-cell power + heat recovery”, we do equipment packaging, system engineering and project investment.

  • Equipment: reforming, PSA, fuel cell and heat recovery integrated into movable, fast-install skids
  • System engineering: multi-energy microgrids for farms, villages and industrial parks — feasibility, applications, construction and commissioning
  • Investment: EMC / EaaS participation, sharing energy savings and revenue with the owner
Technology and partners

Co-developed, made in China

Core process packages and equipment are co-developed and manufactured domestically, without reliance on imported complete units.

Business models

Four ways to work with us

01

Equipment / EPC

Skid supply plus engineering, construction and commissioning for owners who want to build and operate themselves.

02

EMC shared savings

We invest and build, share the energy savings, and transfer the equipment at the end of the contract.

03

EaaS

Pay per kWh of power, heat, cooling or fertiliser — for users with fluctuating demand and no capex appetite.

04

Consulting

Feasibility studies, pilot applications, policy alignment and carbon-asset development for governments and platforms.
