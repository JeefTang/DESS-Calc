Cases and scenarios

We separate what is already running from what is replicable, so you can judge which numbers apply to your project.

DEMONSTRATION · RECOMMENDED

Cold-region pig farm biogas reforming CHP, Baiquanling

A pig farm with more than 9,000 head in Heilongjiang. Manure is digested to biogas and fed to a 120–150 kW CHP unit.

  • About 1.05–1.31 million kWh per year; 150–250 kW recoverable heat
  • Heat returned to the digester; 80–120 kW needed in the coldest month
  • Solves the 70–90% winter gas-output drop in cold-region digesters
  • Recommended by the Heilongjiang Academy of Agricultural Sciences
SCENARIO · REPLICABLE

Oilfield associated gas utilisation

Remote wells, gathering stations and flare gas that were vented or burned. After treatment the gas feeds reforming CHP for site power and heat, replacing diesel gensets.

  • 30–200 Nm³/h, one or two units
  • Heavy hydrocarbon, sulphur and water removal before the reformer
  • Island operation; heat for control rooms and living quarters
  • Can be combined with carbon-asset accounting
SCENARIO · PARK ENERGY STATION

City natural gas distributed CCHP

City gate or park natural gas is reformed to hydrogen for the fuel cell. Power serves buildings, hot water serves domestic and heating loads, with absorption cooling where needed.

  • 100–150 kW modular, staged with load growth
  • Low noise; suitable for park energy stations or rooftops
  • Grid parallel without export, behind-the-meter or self-consumption
  • Can form a microgrid with PV and storage
PARTNER SCENARIO · SOUTHEAST ASIA

POME biogas power, upgrading and BHP

Palm oil mill POME offers large, continuous biogas volume — a good fit for power, upgrading and biogas air conditioning together. The partner manufacturing system has 30 units / 16.4 MW operating in Malaysia.

  • Biogas power 300 kW–2 MW
  • Upgrading to 97% for boilers or bio-CNG
  • BHP for site cooling without grid demand
  • Heat returned to the anaerobic pond

Unit references in the partner scenario come from the partner manufacturing system, not from Huasen-owned projects. Project boundaries and figures are subject to contract and acceptance reports.
