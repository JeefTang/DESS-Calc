Six things for the first conversation

  • Gas source: biogas / coal mine gas / associated gas / natural gas / biomethane
  • Gas data: flow Nm³/h, CH₄%, CO₂%, H₂S ppm
  • Load: electrical kW, hot water or steam, cooling required?
  • Operation: grid parallel, island, export allowed?
  • Site: available area, height, distance to residents
  • Stage: feasibility, approval, tender or site ready
Not sure about gas volume? Send one to two weeks of flow-meter readings or gas-holder records and we can start with a range estimate.
