Four gas sources, one route

Whatever gas you have, we build around it

We are not picky about the source — only about gas quality. First clean the gas to what the reformer accepts, then size hydrogen generation, power and heat recovery to your load.

B

Biogas

Livestock manure, food-processing wastewater, straw, landfill and palm oil mill POME.

Underground extraction gas, gas pumping stations and mine gas power plants.

  • Power-led, heat for mine buildings

Remote wells, gathering stations, vented and flared gas.

  • Heavy hydrocarbon removal, drying
  • Replaces diesel gensets and flaring
N

City natural gas

City gate stations, industrial parks, hotels, hospitals and data centres.

  • CH₄ 90–99%
  • Pressure regulation, fine desulphurisation
  • Cooling, heating and power
How the system works

Five steps from gas to cooling, heat and power

The core equipment is skid-mounted. On site you connect gas, power and water, then commission. Hydrogen is generated on demand and consumed inside the unit — no high-pressure hydrogen storage.

Clean & regulateH₂S down to ≤20 ppm, drying, filtration and stable pressure.
ReformSteam methane reforming at 700–850℃: CH₄ + H₂O → H₂ + CO.
Shift & purifyWater-gas shift + PSA; product hydrogen ≥70%, CO <10 ppm.
Fuel cellPEMFC electrochemical generation — no open flame, low noise.
Heat recovery40–50℃ hot water for digesters, space heating or domestic use.

Power and heat should be counted together

Electrical efficiency is ≥40%; most of the remainder is recovered as useful heat, giving ≥85% overall efficiency. For farms, oilfield stations and industrial parks, heat is often worth more than electricity.

  • Digester heating first — solves winter gas-output collapse in cold regions
  • Hot water, steam and absorption cooling used in load order
  • Grid parallel, island mode and black start available
  • Modular parallel units, expand as gas volume grows

Reference & scenarios

A cold-region pig farm, heated by its own CHP

Baiquanling, Heilongjiang: a pig farm with more than 9,000 head. Manure is digested to biogas and fed to a 120–150 kW reforming fuel-cell CHP unit.

  • About 1.05–1.31 million kWh generated per year; 150–250 kW of recoverable heat
  • Heat returned to the digester; coldest-month demand 80–120 kW
  • Solves the 70–90% winter gas-output drop typical of cold-region digesters
  • Recommended by the Heilongjiang Academy of Agricultural Sciences
