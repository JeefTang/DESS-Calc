Biogas heat-pump air conditioning (BHP)

When the boiler stops, the air conditioning keeps running. A small gas engine drives the compressor directly; the fuel is biogas or biomethane, and there is no electric motor in the drive train.

Why it matters

Break the boiler–turbine–grid dependency

On farms, biogas plants, food factories and industrial parks, cooling often hangs on one chain: boiler stops, turbine stops, site power gets tight, and offices and dormitories lose cooling first. BHP cuts that chain.

  • Gas engine drives an open compressor — no generator, no electric motor
  • 30–480 kW per unit, up to 8 units in parallel to MW scale
  • 97% methane at 5–20 kPa; H₂S <10 ppm
  • Optional engine heat recovery to 60–90℃ hot water
  • Skid-mounted outdoor unit, ground or roof, no plant room
Principle

Gas in, cooling out — and hot water too

This is not an absorption chiller. The engine does mechanical work on the compressor, so one cubic metre of methane yields about 10 kWh of cooling — roughly 50% more than a single-effect absorption unit on the same gas.

Gas inlet97% methane at 5–20 kPa, filtered and regulated.
EngineSpark-ignition gas engine, shaft output via coupling or belt.
CompressionOpen reciprocating or scroll compressor; speed and unloading control capacity.
Heat recoveryJacket water and exhaust heat to 60–90℃ hot water.
COP basis: about 1.0 on gas LHV. On an electricity-equivalent basis the compressor itself is about COP 3.0 — what changes is where the shaft power comes from, not the cooling efficiency.

Applications

Where BHP fits best

Farms and biogas plants

Cool offices, dormitories and laboratories with own biogas; recover heat for the digester or domestic hot water.

Food processing

Slaughterhouses, breweries and produce plants with steady biogas and cooling demand; replaces electric air-con and diesel sets.

Parks and off-grid sites

Weak grids or expensive capacity upgrades; gas cooling solves summer demand without adding electrical load.

Oil and gas stations

Associated or natural gas drives cooling and heating for control rooms, offices and living quarters.

Data and telecom sites

High reliability requirement; BHP does not use grid capacity and can serve as backup cooling.

Hotels and commercial

With the right gas price, BHP provides cooling and hot water and cuts summer electricity peaks.
